## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Wordy is a relaxed word-guessing game with no daily limit. Find the mystery word in six tries, then play another one whenever you like.

Type a word and the tiles show how close you are: green means the letter is in the right place, gold means it is in the word but somewhere else, and gray means it is not in the word at all. Use what you learn to narrow it down.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Choose 4, 5 or 6 letter words
• Unlimited games, no waiting for tomorrow
• Skip any word you do not feel like solving
• Auto-complete fills in letters you have already found

HINTS WHEN YOU NEED THEM
• Reveal the next letter of the word
• Rule out one letter, or every letter that is not in the word
• Your score keeps track of games won with and without hints

LEARN NEW WORDS
• Tap any guess or past answer to read its dictionary definition
• Bookmark words to come back to later
• Search the web for anything the dictionary does not cover

KEEP SCORE
• Earn more points for solving in fewer guesses
• See your total score, average score and win count
• Review every game you have played in your history

The whole game works offline. Only the dictionary lookup needs a connection. There are no ads, no accounts and no in-app purchases.

Wordy is open source: https://github.com/hansololz/wordy
